## What changed and why

- "Transforming the global food system by connecting farmers to wholesalers
  and consumers directly" is the pre-pivot positioning. The pivot card of
  08-08 explains the F moving from Food to Financial; the page contradicted
  it.
- "Food transparency" dropped from the three missions, replaced with custody,
  which is what the product actually differentiates on.
- Pricing stated plainly, matching kits/half-a-percent.jpg: 0.5% to send,
  zero from FAME at the counter. The agent's own fee is deliberately not
  mentioned here, since this is positioning rather than a price table — the
  full journey cost lives on kits/cost-comparison-eur200.png.
- "Decentralized Govenance" was misspelled in the live copy.
- The linktr.ee link is replaced with algofame.org, which is where the
  scheduled cards all point.
